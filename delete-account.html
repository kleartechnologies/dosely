Option 1 — Fastest

Delete directly in the app.

The quickest way to delete your account is directly from Dosely. Follow these steps:

1

Open Dosely

Launch the Dosely app on your iPhone or Android device.

2

Go to Settings

Tap your profile icon in the top corner, then select Settings.

3

Tap "Delete Account"

Scroll to the bottom of Settings and tap Delete Account. You'll be asked to confirm.

4

Confirm deletion

Confirm your choice. Your account and all data will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

Option 2 — Via email

Request deletion by email.

If you can't access the app, email us from the address linked to your Dosely account and we'll delete it for you within 30 days.
